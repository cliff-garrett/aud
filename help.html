Binaural Settings for Deep Awareness & Sensory Comfort

Welcome! This friendly guide is designed to help you configure your custom audio app to achieve a state of detached, deep awareness. These settings are tailored to engage an active mind while soothing your nervous system and managing physical tension.

🧠 1. The Core Binaural Settings for Higher Consciousness

To target the low Theta to upper Delta range for detached awareness while keeping your mind engaged, apply these frequency frameworks:

  • The Carrier Frequency (Pitch): Keep this low—between 100 Hz and 200 Hz. Low, deep pitches mimic vocal chanting, triggering vagal nerve stimulation to soothe tight muscles. Avoid high pitches, which can aggravate tinnitus or trigger sensory defensiveness.
  • The Stereo Frequency Offset (The Beat):
    • For the "Observer" State: Set between 4.0 Hz and 5.5 Hz (Deep Theta). This matches the brain's frequency during deep meditation or high-concept coding, keeping an ADHD mind awake while dropping an Autistic nervous system out of "fight-or-flight."
    • For Deep Sleep Transition: Drop the offset down to 1.5 Hz to 3.0 Hz (Delta range) to quiet your mind for sleep.

🌊 2. Tailoring the Waveforms for Sensory Comfort

The geometry of the sound wave deeply impacts your sensory profile. Choose your shapes carefully:

  • 🟩 The "Go-To" Setting: Triangle Wave. Pure sine waves can sometimes feel sterile or create internal pressure in a hypermobile middle ear. Triangle waves offer a soft texture that gives your brain a pleasant, non-harsh acoustic data point to process.
  • 🟥 What to Avoid: Square Wave. Steer entirely clear of this wave. Its bright, sharp harmonic edges can feel abrasive, trigger sensory defensiveness, and ramp up muscle tension.

🎵 3. Exploiting "Rhythm & Pattern" to Satiate the ADHD Mind

Continuous tones can cause an ADHD brain to become bored and restart internal distractions. Use structured rhythm to keep your dopamine baseline stable:

  • Playback Mode: Select the Overlapping Three-Note Pattern. The predictable pattern satisfies the Autistic need for order, while the overlapping complexity keeps the ADHD mind engaged.
  • The Pacing (Pattern Repeats): Set your repeat cycle to around 1.5s to 2.0s. This structures your nervous system by mimicking a slow, resting human heartbeat or deep meditative breathing.
  • The "Envelope" Settings (Soft Attack/Release): Maximize your Soft Attack (50ms+) and Soft Release (100ms+). This prevents sudden audio clicking or popping, ensuring tones gently bloom and fade so your muscles remain relaxed.

  • Carrier Frequency: 150 Hz
  • Offset Frequency: 4.5 Hz
  • Waveform: Triangle
  • Pattern Mode: Overlapping three-note pattern
  • Pitch Variation: 10 Hz (Subtle and gentle)
